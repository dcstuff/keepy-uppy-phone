Keepy-Uppy Phone

Your screen is the paddle. Keep the ball bouncing on it.

  1. Hold the phone flat, screen up.
  2. Flick it upward to launch the ball: a short, sharp pop that stops dead, like tossing a pancake.
  3. Follow the shadow. If the ball drifts off, the arrow shows where it is.
  4. Lift the phone into the ball as it lands for a bigger bounce. Faster impacts score more.
  5. The meter at the bottom shows how hard you flicked, and why a flick didn't count.
